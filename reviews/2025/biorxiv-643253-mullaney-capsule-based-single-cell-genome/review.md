### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

This manuscript characterizes a commercially available semi-permeable capsule (SPC) composed of an acrylate-substituted polysaccharide shell and a dextran core (mean diameter $82.9\ \mu\text{m}$, tunable from $40.4\ \mu\text{m}$ to $182.6\ \mu\text{m}$) for compartmentalized cell culture and single-cell genomics. Using DNA ladders, native protein standards, ribosomes, mononucleosomes, and *de novo* designed protein oligomers (6-mer to 60-mer), the authors map size-selective retention across the hydrogel shell, showing that static DNA fragments $>300\text{ bp}$ and large nucleoprotein complexes are retained while oligonucleotides, enzymes, and IgG antibodies diffuse across the membrane. They apply SPCs to four experimental workflows: (1) high-throughput encapsulation and culture of *Escherichia coli* ($525,000$ loaded SPCs at $21\%$ occupancy) and mammalian cell lines (K562, Jurkat, Nalm6, and HEK293T, including cryopreservation); (2) in-capsule PCR and isothermal primary template amplification (PTA); (3) four-round ligation-based combinatorial indexing for targeted 4-gene single-cell amplicon sequencing ($387$ cells) and human-mouse species-mixing single-cell whole-genome sequencing (sc-WGS; mean $1,433,349$ reads/cell) using PTA or modified multiple displacement amplification (mMDA); and (4) a proof-of-concept lineage tracing experiment sequencing $1,000$ serially bottlenecked K562 cells engineered with the hypermutator polymerase $\varepsilon$ allele (`POLE P286R`). Raw sequencing data, custom barcode-processing and phylogenetic scripts, Supplementary Tables 1–3, and the majority of experimental and computational Methods sections are currently absent from the manuscript, with the Data Availability statement noting that links will be added in a future update.

The physical characterization of SPC chemical stability and biomolecular permeability using atomically defined protein oligomers is well executed, and the authors transparently document amplicon leakage into and out of capsules during PCR and isothermal amplification (Figs. S10–S12). However, several quantitative and experimental flaws undermine the main biological and genomic claims. First, the claim that SPCs support "clonally isolated cellular cultures" for mammalian cells (Abstract, Introduction, and line 56) is contradicted by the manuscript's own viability data: encapsulated single K562 cells died by day 8 ($44.5\%$ occupancy; Fig. S6A), and sustained mammalian growth required high-density multiplet encapsulation ($69.16\%$ occupancy, $32.9\%$ multiplet rate) in conditioned media, where initial cell counts dropped due to single-cell death and selection for multi-cell capsules (lines 50–56). Second, the reported sc-WGS Gini coefficients of $0.236$ (mMDA) and $0.277$ (PTA) across $1\text{ kb}$ genomic bins at a mean depth of $1.43 \times 10^6$ reads/cell (line 76; Fig. 3I) are mathematically impossible over the full genome ($\sim 3 \times 10^6$ bins of $1\text{ kb}$, or $\le 0.48$ reads/bin on average, where $\ge 62\%$ of $1\text{ kb}$ bins must contain zero reads even under an ideal uniform Poisson process, yielding a theoretical Gini $>0.70$), indicating that zero-read bins were excluded or a much larger bin size was used. Third, the `POLE P286R` lineage tracing experiment was initiated from $3\text{--}5$ founding cells rather than a single clone (line 93), confounding *de novo* replication-coupled mutagenesis with pre-existing clonal heterogeneity and drift among founders, while pairwise single-cell distances rely on naive Hamming distances across sparse shallow-WGS calls (`AD >= 2`) without accounting for single-cell allelic dropout. These issues can be resolved through complete reporting of the missing Methods and Supplementary Tables, recomputing genome-wide Lorenz curves across all genomic bins (including zero-count bins), and re-analyzing the single-cell somatic variant matrix with explicit dropout modeling and founder-clone deconvolution.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **Polysaccharide-shell SPCs withstand harsh chemical/thermal treatments and exhibit size-selective permeability to DNA, proteins, and *de novo* oligomers: [Well Supported].**  
  Brightfield and SEM imaging, overnight exposures to organic solvents, extreme pH ($6\text{ M HCl}$ to $6\text{ M NaOH}$), temperatures from $-196^\circ\text{C}$ to $100^\circ\text{C}$ (Fig. 1B,C; Fig. S1), and serial wash retention assays with DNA ladders, protein standards, ribosomes, mononucleosomes, GFP-tagged *de novo* oligomers, and anti-CD19-PE antibodies (Fig. 1D–G; Figs. S3–S5) consistently establish the physical stability and size-selective diffusion cutoff of the capsules under non-amplifying conditions.

* **SPCs support high-throughput clonal bacterial culture, whereas mammalian cell growth in SPCs requires multi-cell encapsulation and conditioned media rather than clonal single-cell isolation: [Overstated].**  
  Clonal bacterial growth is well demonstrated by encapsulating a 50:50 mixture of eGFP- and mScarlet-expressing *E. coli* at sub-Poisson loading ($21\%$ occupancy, $2.37\%$ expected multiplet rate), yielding $11.3\%$ mScarlet-only, $13.8\%$ eGFP-only, and $3.2\%$ mixed capsules with a $20\text{ min}$ doubling time (Fig. 2B–D). By contrast, when mammalian K562 cells were encapsulated at moderate density ($44.5\%$ occupancy) in fresh media, viability dropped by $72\%$ at day 4 and nearly all cells died by day 8 (line 50; Fig. S6A). Mammalian proliferation required loading at $69.16\%$ occupancy ($32.9\%$ multiplet rate) in conditioned media, and the authors explicitly note for Nalm6, Jurkat, and HEK293T that initial cell count declines reflect "the death of single cells isolated within SPCs and the selection for cells within SPCs containing multiple cells" (line 56). Describing these mammalian cultures as "clonally isolated cellular cultures" (line 56; Abstract line 7) is unsupported by the data.

* **Combinatorial indexing in SPCs achieves sensitive targeted amplicon sequencing with low cross-capsule allele swapping despite measurable amplicon diffusion during amplification: [Moderately Supported].**  
  The authors show that although static DNA $>300\text{ bp}$ is retained during washing (Fig. 1D), active PCR and $30^\circ\text{C}$ isothermal PTA cause DNA fragments from $100\text{ bp}$ to $1,500\text{ bp}$ to leak into the supernatant (Figs. S10–S11), and extracellular amplicons of $100\text{--}500\text{ bp}$ can enter empty capsules during bulk PCR (Fig. S12). In the 4-gene multiplex PCR assay ($460\text{--}2,155\text{ bp}$) across K562:PBMC mixtures ($387$ cells, mean $1,008$ reads/cell), all 4 targets were detected in $96.9\%$ of cells (Fig. 3D), both alleles of the heterozygous `AKT3` p.G37Ter locus were recovered in $84.91\%$ of K562 cells (Fig. 3F), and K562 cells in the 10:90 and 20:80 mixtures maintained $99.2\%$ ($39$ intact-amplicon cells) and $100\%$ ($20$ fragmented-amplicon cells) allelic purity at `FANCC` chr9:95,247,502 (Fig. 3E). However, cross-contamination is only reported at `FANCC` within the minority K562 population ($39$ and $20$ cells); allelic purity in the majority PBMC population ($80\text{--}90\%$ of capsules), contamination rates across the shorter amplicons in the panel (which are most prone to diffusion in Fig. S12), and background read counts in empty barcodes are not quantified.

* **SPC-based sc-WGS with mMDA and PTA yields highly uniform whole-genome coverage with Gini coefficients of 0.236 and 0.277 across 1 kb bins at 1.43 million reads per cell: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  At a mean sequencing depth of $1,433,349$ reads/cell (line 76), a $3\text{ Gb}$ mammalian genome divided into $3 \times 10^6$ bins of $1\text{ kb}$ has an average of $\lambda \approx 0.48$ reads per $1\text{ kb}$ bin (or $\sim 0.24$ read pairs/bin). Even under an idealized, perfectly uniform Poisson distribution across the genome, a fraction $e^{-\lambda} \ge 62\%$ of all $1\text{ kb}$ bins must have zero reads, which mathematically forces the genome-wide Gini coefficient across all $1\text{ kb}$ bins to exceed $0.62$ (specifically, $G \approx 0.72$ for $\text{Poisson}(0.48)$). Achieving Gini coefficients of $0.236$ and $0.277$ (line 76; Fig. 3I; Fig. S13B) is only possible if empty ($0$-read) $1\text{ kb}$ bins were dropped prior to computing the Lorenz curve or if a much larger bin size ($\ge 50\text{--}100\text{ kb}$) was used. Omitting zero-count bins at $\sim 0.05\times$ depth renders the Gini coefficient insensitive to locus dropout. In addition, the total number of human and mouse cells sequenced, the species doublet rate, and the per-cell distribution of cross-species contaminating reads in Figure 3G are not reported in the text.

* **Shallow SPC sc-WGS of 1,000 `POLE P286R` cells faithfully reconstructs continuous division-coupled cellular lineages: [Fragile].**  
  While CRISPR-Cas9 introduction of `POLE P286R` into K562 cells elevates the characteristic $\text{TCT}>\text{TTT}$ mutational signature (Fig. 4B,C), the bulk time-course regression shows only moderate linearity ($862$ mutations per million reads per day, Pearson's $r = 0.58$, $r^2 = 0.34$; line 87) without confidence intervals or $p$-values. In the single-cell lineage tracing experiment (Fig. 4D–H), founding each bottleneck series with "approximately 3–5 cells" (line 93) from a bulk-edited culture ($\sim 50\%$ editing efficiency; Fig. S14) rather than a single clonal cell means that population splits can sort pre-existing founder lineages and copy-number variants rather than purely *de novo* post-split mutations—consistent with the failure of population P2 to cluster according to the experimental split tree (Fig. 4G). Furthermore, the reported variant totals ($1,379,244$ total variants with $\text{AD} \ge 2$ versus a mean of $954$ variants/cell across $1,000$ cells, where $1,000 \times 954 = 954,000 < 1,379,244$; line 93) are arithmetically incompatible unless additional cells or singleton/low-AD calls were included in the total, and pairwise normalized Hamming distances on shallow sc-WGS data do not account for high false-negative allelic dropout rates at $2\times$ coverage.

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Recompute sc-WGS Coverage Uniformity (Lorenz Curves and Gini Coefficients) Across All Reference Bins and Report Species-Mixing Metrics
* **WHY:** Dropping zero-count $1\text{ kb}$ bins at $\sim 1.43 \times 10^6$ reads/cell ($\sim 0.05\times$ genome depth) artificially depresses the Gini coefficient from $>0.70$ to $0.236\text{--}0.277$ and masks whole-locus amplification dropout.
* **WHAT:** Recompute Lorenz curves and Gini coefficients across all non-gap reference genome bins (including zero-read bins) at $10\text{ kb}$, $50\text{ kb}$, and $100\text{ kb}$ resolutions, compare them directly against an analytical or simulated Poisson baseline matched to each cell's exact mapped read count, and report quantitative species-mixing statistics for Figure 3G.
* **HOW:**
  1. For each cell in the mMDA and PTA libraries (Fig. 3G–I), tile the mappable primary chromosomes of `hg38` and `mm10` (excluding N-gaps and ENCODE blacklist regions) into fixed windows of $1\text{ kb}$, $10\text{ kb}$, and $100\text{ kb}$, retaining all $0$-count windows.
  2. Compute both the raw Gini coefficient $G_{\text{raw}}$ (including $0$-count bins) and the depth-normalized Gini coefficient $G / G_{\text{Poisson}}(N_{\text{reads}})$, alongside the fraction of genome covered at $\ge 1$ read per cell.
  3. In the text for Figure 3G, state the exact number of recovered human cells, mouse cells, and human-mouse multiplets for both mMDA and PTA, and report the median and interquartile range (IQR) of heterologous (cross-species) read fractions per cell.

#### 2. Account for Founder-Clone Multiplicity, Coverage Sparsity, and Allelic Dropout in the `POLE P286R` Lineage Analysis
* **WHY:** Starting serial bottlenecks with $3\text{--}5$ cells from a heterogeneously edited K562 pool confounds pre-existing clonal variation with *de novo* `POLE P286R` mutagenesis, and naive pairwise Hamming distances at shallow sc-WGS depth (`AD >= 2`) conflate missing/dropout alleles with reference alleles. Additionally, the reported $1,379,244$ total variants exceed the maximum possible sum of $954$ variants/cell across $1,000$ cells ($954,000$).
* **WHAT:** Reconcile the variant count arithmetic, quantify the distribution of pairwise jointly callable sites across the $1,000$ cells, separate pre-existing founder variants from subclonal post-bottleneck mutations, and evaluate tree topology stability under a dropout-aware likelihood model.
* **HOW:**
  1. State the exact number of sequenced cells passing QC, mean reads and genomic breadth per cell, and the exact filtering criteria producing the $1,379,244$ total variants versus the $954$ variants/cell mean in Figure 4F.
  2. Define a site as jointly callable between cell $i$ and cell $j$ only when both cells have total read depth $\text{DP} \ge 4$ (or report a sensitivity sweep over $\text{DP} \ge 2, 3, 5$), and report the median number of jointly callable variant sites per cell pair used to construct Figure 4H.
  3. Identify founder-defining somatic variants (variants shared across major early branches or present in bulk baseline K562 controls) to test whether the $1,000$ cells partition into $3\text{--}5$ founding clones and whether population P2 represents selective expansion of an unedited or distinct founder clone.
  4. Compare the normalized Hamming distance dendrogram against a single-cell phylogenetic model that explicitly parameterizes per-site allelic dropout rate $\beta$ and amplification false-positive rate $\alpha$ (e.g., `CellPhy` or `SCITE` on the informative shared variant subset), reporting bootstrap support for the population nodes in Figure 4G.

#### 3. Quantify Amplicon Cross-Contamination Across All Four Loci and Both Cell Types, and Explain Amplification-Induced Leakage
* **WHY:** Supplementary Figures S10–S12 show that $100\text{--}1,500\text{ bp}$ amplicons exit SPCs during PCR/PTA and $100\text{--}500\text{ bp}$ amplicons can enter empty SPCs, yet Figure 3E only reports allelic purity for `FANCC` in the minority K562 cells ($n = 39$ and $n = 20$).
* **WHAT:** Report bidirectional cross-contamination across both K562 and PBMC capsules and across all informative SNP loci in the 4-gene panel (`AKT3`, `BIRC6`, `FANCC`, `BRCA1`), ordered by amplicon length ($460\text{ bp}$ to $2,155\text{ bp}$), and discuss the biophysical mechanism of amplification-associated leakage.
* **HOW:**
  1. Using existing BAM/GVCF files from the 100:0, 20:80, and 10:90 K562:PBMC mixes, tabulate the fraction of contaminating alternate (`T`) reads at `FANCC` chr9:95,247,502 inside the PBMC cells ($n \approx 328$ cells) as well as reference (`C`) reads inside the K562 cells, alongside any other cell-line-distinguishing SNPs present across the $460\text{--}2,155\text{ bp}$ amplicons.
  2. Discuss why DNA up to $1,500\text{ bp}$ leaks into the supernatant during PCR and $30^\circ\text{C}$ PTA (Figs. S10–S11) whereas static DNA $>300\text{ bp}$ is completely retained (Fig. 1D)—for example, testing or discussing whether Pluronic F-68 / alkaline lysis / $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ alters hydrogel mesh size or whether active polymerase strand displacement threads nascent ssDNA/dsDNA through the polysaccharide pores.

#### 4. Align Mammalian Cell Culture Claims with the Multiplet Encapsulation Data
* **WHY:** Claiming that SPCs support "clonally isolated cellular cultures" across mammalian cell lines misrepresents experiments in which single cells died ($72\%$ loss by day 4 and near-complete death by day 8 at $44.5\%$ occupancy) and growth was rescued only by encapsulating multiple cells per capsule ($69.16\%$ occupancy, $32.9\%$ multiplet rate) in conditioned media.
* **WHAT:** Revise the Abstract, Introduction, Results, and Discussion to distinguish clonal single-cell bacterial growth from multi-cell mammalian spheroid/colony culture.
* **HOW:**
  1. In the Abstract (line 7), Introduction (line 17), and Results conclusion (line 56), specify that single-cell clonal expansion was demonstrated in bacteria (*E. coli*), whereas mammalian cell lines (K562, Jurkat, Nalm6, HEK293T) required paracrine conditioning and multi-cell co-encapsulation ($\sim 33\%$ multiplet loading) to overcome single-cell death.
  2. If clonal mammalian expansion is claimed, perform a dual-color (e.g., eGFP/mScarlet) mammalian mixing experiment analogous to Figure 2C,D to quantify the fraction of surviving day-8 mammalian colonies that are monochromatic versus polyclonal.
